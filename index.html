    West Harbor · San Pedro

    Harbor Sweep

    Trash washes out of storm drains and drifts toward the ocean. Pilot the robot that scoops it up first. Steer it yourself, then plan a route and let it drive alone.

    About 2 minutes. Student concept by a USC WRIT 340 ATLAS team for Clean Earth Rovers.

    Level 1 of 2

    Remote control

    • 1Drag anywhere to steer. Arrow keys work too.
    • 2Scoop trash with the open front before the current carries it out to sea.
    • 3The basket holds 10 pieces. Empty it at the catch bin on the dock.
    • 4Give room to boats and sea lions. Bumping them costs you time.

    You have 40 seconds.

    Real operators steer the rover the same way, standing on the dock with a handheld controller.

    Level 2 of 2

    Autopilot

    • 1Draw a route from the rover. Drag, or tap to drop waypoints.
    • 2Dotted trails show where the current will carry each piece. Numbers on your route show when the rover gets there.
    • 3Launch. The rover drives alone and stops by itself for boats and wildlife.
